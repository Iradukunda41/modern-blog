Easy Chocolate Cake

Oct 1, 2026 6 min read

Introduction

Easy Chocolate Cake is a soft and delicious dessert that is perfect for birthdays, family gatherings and special moments.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • Vanilla extract

Preparation

  1. Mix the flour, cocoa powder and baking powder.
  2. Add sugar and mix well.
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil and vanilla.
  4. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into a prepared baking pan.
  6. Bake until the cake is fully cooked.
  7. Allow it to cool before serving.

Tips

Make sure all ingredients are well mixed before baking. You can decorate the cake with fruit or a light topping.
