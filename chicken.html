Perfect Grilled Chicken

Oct 2, 2026 7 min read

Introduction

Grilled chicken is a delicious and simple meal that can be enjoyed with vegetables, salad or other side dishes.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Fresh herbs
  • Lemon juice

Preparation

  1. Clean and prepare the chicken.
  2. Mix the oil, garlic, lemon juice and seasonings.
  3. Coat the chicken with the mixture.
  4. Allow the chicken to absorb the flavors.
  5. Grill until fully cooked.
  6. Serve with fresh vegetables or salad.

Tips

Allow the chicken to rest for a few minutes before serving. This helps keep the meat tender and flavorful.
