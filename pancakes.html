Fluffy Breakfast Pancakes

Sep 29, 2026 5 min read

Introduction

Fluffy breakfast pancakes are an easy and delicious way to start the day. They can be served with fruit or other toppings of your choice.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • A pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  2. Add the eggs and milk.
  3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled pan.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan.
  6. Cook both sides until golden.
  7. Serve warm with your favorite topping.

Tips

Avoid overmixing the batter because this can make the pancakes less fluffy. Serve them while warm.
