Fresh Garden Salad

Oct 3, 2026 4 min read

Introduction

Fresh Garden Salad is a simple and colorful meal made with fresh vegetables. It is easy to prepare and can be enjoyed as a light meal or as a side dish.

Ingredients

  • Fresh lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Preparation

  1. Wash all the vegetables carefully.
  2. Cut the lettuce, tomatoes and cucumber.
  3. Grate the carrot.
  4. Slice the onion into small pieces.
  5. Mix all vegetables in a large bowl.
  6. Add olive oil and salt.
  7. Mix gently and serve fresh.

Tips

Use fresh vegetables for better taste and texture. You can also add other vegetables that you enjoy.
